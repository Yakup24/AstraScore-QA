# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, focus on technical merit, and keep collaboration professional.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discriminatory language
- Publishing private information
- Sharing credentials or sensitive customer data
- Disruptive or intentionally misleading contributions

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard.
